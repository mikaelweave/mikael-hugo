# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Readers of mikael.dev: developers, technical peers, and future collaborators who want practical notes from Mikael Weaver's engineering work. They are usually reading a specific article or reference while solving a real setup, cloud, tooling, or development workflow problem.

## Product Purpose

mikael.dev is a personal technical website for publishing practical engineering notes, experiments, photography, and project write-ups. Success means visitors quickly understand the author, trust the technical guidance, and can apply the instructions without wading through unnecessary polish or marketing language.

## Brand Personality

Clear, pragmatic, and personal. The site should feel like a knowledgeable engineer sharing field-tested notes: direct enough to copy from, warm enough to read, and opinionated enough to be memorable.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS landing-page polish, AI-looking card grids, decorative developer-terminal cosplay, and visual treatments that make technical instructions harder to scan. The site should not look like a product dashboard unless a page is explicitly an interactive tool.

## Design Principles

- Make technical work copyable: commands, code, and setup steps should be easy to find, select, and reuse.
- Preserve author voice: prefer concise first-person context over anonymous documentation tone.
- Keep pages readable first: interaction should enhance long-form content, not replace it.
- Let practical detail carry authority: avoid filler visuals when specific notes, links, or commands help more.
- Use the existing identity before inventing a new one: extend the site's current Hugo, SCSS, and earth-tone palette.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast and keyboard-accessible interactions. Interactive pages should preserve useful no-JavaScript content, expose status changes with accessible text, keep focus visible, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
